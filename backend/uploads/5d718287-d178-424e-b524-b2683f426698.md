# The Quantum Coffee Machine
The Quantum Coffee Machine was invented by Dr. Emmett Brown in 2025. It uses quantum entanglement to brew coffee simultaneously across multiple parallel universes. This ensures that in at least one universe, the coffee is perfectly brewed and never burnt. The machine requires a stable power source of 1.21 gigawatts, typically provided by a localized fusion reactor.

## Key Features
- **Entanglement Brewing:** Beans are ground in a superposition of states.
- **Multiverse Selection:** Only the best tasting coffee collapses into the final cup.
- **Wormhole Dispenser:** Coffee is delivered instantly to your mug, regardless of distance.
