# The Mars Terraforming Protocol
Initiated in 2042 by the Global Space Coalition (GSC), the Mars Terraforming Protocol aims to create a breathable atmosphere on Mars within 100 years. The project is led by Commander Sarah Jenkins.

## Stages of Terraforming
1. **Atmospheric Thickening:** Using orbital mirrors to melt the polar ice caps, releasing trapped CO2.
2. **Temperature Stabilization:** Deploying dark dust across the Martian surface to absorb sunlight.
3. **Biological Introduction:** Planting genetically modified lichens and cyanobacteria to produce oxygen.

The total budget for the protocol is estimated at 5 trillion Galactic Credits.
